05 / Scope

Boundaries and risks

What ships now, what waits, and what could go wrong.

Included

  • Future-state wide and narrow preview
  • Continuity hierarchy and copy
  • Responsive launch rendering
  • Lifecycle ownership checks
  • Automated and live verification

Deferred

  • Permanent dashboard wall
  • Mouse interaction
  • Composer-conflicting global shortcuts
  • New UI dependencies
The wide wordmark leaves only four state lines.

Improve hierarchy with markers, alignment, color, and copy rather than adding rows.

The continuity spine could become decorative noise.

Show it only at 72 columns and above; keep narrow layouts plain.

Copper could become ambient decoration.

Reserve copper for identity, the local spine, and NEXT.

Terminal rendering can differ from string fixtures.

Require live checks after deterministic harness verification.
