## Why

- **Model line:** a one-time setup fact, repeated in `/status`.
- **Review note:** it warned about unreviewed changes on a turn that changed nothing. It stays for real edits, because silently skipping review there would mislead you.
- **Status block:** `/mode`, `/model`, `/thinking` and `/new` already confirm their own changes.

## Kept on purpose

- Logo and launch status: they show which workspace you are in, once.
- `[tool]` lines, approvals, errors and review results: these are safety output.
- `Neura closed.` on exit: it is not part of a reply.
- `[reasoning summary]`: harder prompts can still show it. `/thinking off` or `--no-thinking` hides it.
